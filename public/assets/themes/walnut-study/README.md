# Walnut Study assets

`board-surface.webp` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-09-08 and encoded as WebP (quality 88, 1536 × 1024, 231,220 bytes). No source photograph was used. The rejected leather table with a perimeter rim is not included in this theme.

`card-back.svg` is original project vector artwork. Slot SVGs are produced by `src/theme/SlotArtwork.ts` from the theme palette; they use vector suit paths rather than fonts.

## Final image prompt

Production background for a solitaire game, full bleed landscape 1536x1024. Perfectly orthographic straight-down view of a continuous dark warm walnut wood surface extending infinitely beyond all four sides of the picture. Quiet elegant aged polished walnut, fine natural longitudinal wood grain at SMALL scale, subtly varied rich espresso and muted brown, matte soft satin finish, restrained broad patina and warm diffuse ambient light. The wood is the entire image, right to all four edges. No visible limits of the table. NO rim, NO border, NO frame, NO edge, NO trim, NO inlay, NO stitching, NO leather, NO felt, NO panels, NO inset, NO seams, NO separated planks, NO decorative lines. No cards, no slots, no symbols, no objects, no text. Calm low contrast tactile grain with no prominent knots, no dramatic stripe, no grunge, no scratches, not rustic barnwood. Surface detail fine and understated so bright cream playing cards will be instantly readable over it. Slight broad diffuse warm illumination around upper center, only subtly darker elsewhere, no black vignette. Photoreal refined game material, premium quietly lived-in wood, uninterrupted unframed infinite surface.
